Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This Policy explains how The Reality Index Publisher handles information used to connect social media accounts and prepare, approve, schedule, and publish educational videos.

1. Information involved

Depending on the platform permissions you grant and features you use, the Publisher may process:

  • Connected account identifiers, usernames, and limited profile information;
  • Authorization tokens and related connection information;
  • Videos, thumbnails, captions, descriptions, scheduling preferences, and approval decisions;
  • Upload identifiers, processing status, error messages, and publishing history;
  • Basic performance statistics where analytics access has been authorized.

The public website is a static GitHub Pages site. GitHub and any network infrastructure used to serve it may collect technical access information under their own policies.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts; prepare and transfer content; carry out user-approved publishing actions; manage schedules and upload states; display analytics where authorized; diagnose errors; and maintain the safety and reliability of the Publisher.

3. Connected platforms and service providers

To perform requested actions, the Publisher transmits relevant account and content data to connected platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube through their APIs. Video media may be made temporarily accessible via a controlled HTTPS delivery link where a platform needs to retrieve it. The Service may also use local hosting, network-tunneling infrastructure, and tools selected to create or process videos. Those providers process information under their own terms and policies.

4. Authorization and control

Connected account permissions are granted through the relevant platform’s authorization process. You can review or revoke an app’s access through that platform’s account settings. Revocation stops future authorized API operations once the Service recognizes it, but does not automatically remove material already published on the platform.

5. Storage and security

The Publisher is currently operated using a self-hosted workflow and publishing environment. Account credentials and publishing records are intended to be stored separately from publicly accessible website files. Reasonable safeguards such as access controls and limited-scope media links are used, but no storage or transfer method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Connected account data, generated content, and publishing records may be retained while needed to operate the Service, preserve upload history, prevent duplicate publishing, or meet legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of data held by The Reality Index Publisher, email therealityindex52@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and handled as applicable. Deleting local records does not remove posts or data independently retained by a connected social media platform.

7. Sharing and sale of data

The Publisher is not designed to sell connected-account information or use it for third-party targeted advertising. Information is disclosed to service providers or social platforms as necessary to perform requested operations, for security, or where legally required.

8. Children's privacy

The Publisher is intended for authorized account managers and is not designed for children. Anyone who believes a child has provided personal information to the Service may contact the address below.

9. International processing

Connected platforms and infrastructure providers may process information in countries outside your location, subject to their own data handling practices and applicable law.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when features, platform integrations, or data practices change. The updated version and effective date will be posted here.

11. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data requests, email therealityindex52@gmail.com.
